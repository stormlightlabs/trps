In this section we look at how the cache behaves after a restart.

The cache stays cold until the first request arrives, and every entry is
fetched again from the origin.

As we have seen, warming is a traffic problem rather than a storage problem.

A restart empties the cache, so the cache spends the quiet hours empty and
the busy hours filling.

The cache is empty after a restart, which means the cache fills during the
busy hours instead of the quiet ones.

Filling the cache during the busy hours is what a restart costs, because the
cache is empty and the busy hours are when the cache fills.

In summary, nothing warms the cache except traffic.
